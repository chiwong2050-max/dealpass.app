# DealPass - Executive Summary

## The Opportunity
Local businesses struggle to fill "off-peak" capacity (e.g., Tuesday nights, weekday mornings). Digital advertising (Google/Facebook) is expensive and risky because you pay for clicks, not customers.
Simultaneously, consumers are facing cost-of-living pressures and are actively seeking meaningful savings, not just "5% off".

## The Solution: DealPass
A mobile-first membership platform connecting high-intent shoppers with local businesses.
*   **For Businesses:** A "Pay-for-Performance" marketing tool. List exclusive offers for off-peak times. Zero upfront cost.
*   **For Users:** A digital club card unlocking 20-50% off dining, entertainment, and services.

## Business Model
1.  **Business Subscriptions:**
    *   Free Trial (Acquisition)
    *   $29/month Pro Plan (Recurring Revenue)
    *   Featured Listings & Boosts (Upsell)
2.  **User Memberships:**
    *   Freemium model (Ad-supported, limited unlocks)
    *   $4.99/month Premium (Recurring Revenue, 100% margin)

## Go-To-Market Strategy
*   **Hyper-Local Launch:** Focus on one city district at a time (e.g., Melbourne CBD) to build density.
*   **Agent Network:** "City Managers" recruit businesses on a commission-only basis, minimizing fixed overhead.
*   **Viral Loops:** "Give 2, Get 1" referral program for users.

## Competitive Advantage
*   **No Hardware:** 100% smartphone-based redemption (QR codes).
*   **Better Economics:** Unlike Groupon (50% commission), DealPass takes **0% commission** on the sale.
*   **Local Focus:** Built for neighborhood discovery, not e-commerce.

## Financial Projections (Year 1 Target)
*   **Active Cities:** 5
*   **Partner Businesses:** 2,000
*   **Premium Members:** 10,000
*   **Projected ARR:** ~$800k - $1M

## Capital Requirement
Seeking initial seed funding to accelerate the "City Manager" program and technical development of the geolocation engine.
